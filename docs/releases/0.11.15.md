SoundPrep 0.11.15

The tracks SoundPrep adds for its preview chains are now named SoundPrep instead of AM, the old AuditionMacro name. The input track, the Raw track and each chain track now read SoundPrep • Preview input, SoundPrep • Raw and SoundPrep • followed by the chain name.

Existing projects keep working exactly as before, because SoundPrep recognises its tracks by an internal marker, not by name. Saved projects keep their current track names; new projects get the new ones.

SoundPrep is free and open source. If it saves you time, you can support development on Ko-fi: https://ko-fi.com/kuma_pontakuaudio
